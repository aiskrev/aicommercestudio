# Business plan (working draft)

This is a structured planning document. It contains **no invented figures, clients or results** —
every number that the business must decide is marked `[to define]`.

## 1. Positioning

**A.I. Commerce Studio** — the digital platform and technology brand of **Iskrev Consulting Ltd**
(Founder & CEO: Aleksandar Iskrev) — an e-commerce engineering and growth studio that
combines store maintenance, SEO, data, integrations and applied AI for small and medium businesses
in Bulgaria and the EU.

Core message: one technical partner for the store, the data and AI — with measurable goals,
transparent process and human control over anything with real-world consequences.

## 2. Target customers

| Segment | Typical trigger | Entry offer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small/medium online stores | instability, slow site, no in-house developer | maintenance retainer, technical audit |
| Shopify merchants | app sprawl, performance, integrations | maintenance, integrations |
| WooCommerce merchants | updates, plugin conflicts, security | maintenance, security hardening |
| Local retailers going online | need a plan and stock integration | technical audit, integrations |
| Companies seeking automation | manual work between e-mail, CRM, accounting | automation project |
| Small businesses wanting custom AI | concrete AI use case | fixed-price AI project |
| Businesses needing maintenance + SEO | stagnant organic traffic | SEO retainer |

## 3. Offer

Six service lines (maintenance; SEO; custom AI tools; AI process automation; analytics & CRO;
API integrations, cloud & security) plus the **paid AI & e-commerce technical audit** as the
recommended entry point.

## 5. Sales process

Inquiry (website) → qualification call → audit or scoped proposal → written offer → delivery in
iterations → measurement → retainer/support. The admin panel tracks this as
`NEW → CONTACTED → QUALIFIED → PROPOSAL → WON/LOST`.
